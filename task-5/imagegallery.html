12 Of The Most Beautiful Places In India You Have To See In 2026 To Believe!



Yumthang Valley, Sikkim – The Bowl Of Flowers

On top of our list of the beautiful places in India is Yumthang Valley, the bottomless vale with a remarkable variety of flowers to boast of. It offers an eye-catching vista of peaks along with Pauhunri and Shundu Tsenpa. The valley is an amazing treat where you would become the element of the ceaseless picture exclusive of any sort of infringement.

Places to Stay: Yarlam Resort, Apply Valley Inn

Things To Do: Visit the beautiful town of Lachen, Explore the Shingba Rhododendron Sanctuary, Spend time near the Yumthang Chu, Visit the Zero Point

Best Time to Visit:September to December

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Bagdogra Airport, which is around 124 km from the valley. The nearest railway station is New Jalpaiguri Railway Station, which is around 120 km from the valley.

Stok Kangri, Ladakh – The Paradise For Trekkers

Stok Kangri is a mountain in the Stok Range of the Himalayas. It is located in the Ladakh region of India and has an elevation of 6,153 meters (20,187 feet). The mountain is a popular destination for trekkers and mountaineers, offering stunning views of the surrounding landscape. The trek to Stok Kangri is considered challenging and requires a good level of fitness, but it rewards adventurers with breathtaking scenery and a sense of accomplishment upon reaching the summit.

Places to Stay: Stok Palace, Ladakh Sarai

Things To Do: Visit the Stok Monastery, Explore the Stok Village, Trek to the Stok Kangri Summit, Enjoy the scenic beauty of the surrounding mountains

Best Time to Visit: June to September

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport in Leh, which is around 15 km from Stok. The nearest railway station is Jammu Tawi Railway Station, which is around 700 km from Stok.

Nubra Valley, Ladakh – The Undiscovered Realm Of Nature

Nubra Valley is a valley in the Ladakh region of India. It is located in the Nubra district and is known for its stunning landscapes and unique culture. The valley is home to several monasteries and offers breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains. Visitors can explore the valley on foot or by vehicle, and it's a great place to experience the local lifestyle and traditions.

Places to Stay: Nubra Valley Resort, Leh Guest House

Things To Do: Visit the Diskit Monastery, Explore the Nubra Valley, Take a camel ride through the sand dunes, Visit the Hunder Sand Dunes

Best Time to Visit: May to September

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport in Leh, which is around 15 km from Nubra Valley. The nearest railway station is Jammu Tawi Railway Station, which is around 700 km from Nubra Valley.

Nohkalikai Falls, Cherrapunji – Cascading Water That Changes Colour

Nohkalikai Falls is a waterfall located in Cherrapunji, Meghalaya, India. It is one of the tallest waterfalls in India, with a height of approximately 340 meters (1,115 feet). The waterfall is known for its stunning beauty and the unique phenomenon of changing colors. The water appears to change color due to the presence of minerals and the way sunlight interacts with the water. Nohkalikai Falls is a popular tourist destination and offers breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape.

Places to Stay: Cherrapunji Holiday Resort, Pinewood Hotel

Things To Do: Visit the Nohkalikai Falls, Explore the Living Root Bridges, Take a walk through the Mawkdok Dympep Valley, Visit the Mawsmai Cave

Best Time to Visit: September to May

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Shillong Airport, which is around 85 km from Cherrapunji. The nearest railway station is Guwahati Railway Station, which is around 150 km from Cherrapunji.

Andaman And Nicobar Islands – The Undisturbed Honeymooners’ Getaway

The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are a group of islands located in the Bay of Bengal, India. They are known for their pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, and rich biodiversity. The islands offer a perfect getaway for honeymooners and nature lovers alike. Visitors can enjoy activities such as snorkeling, scuba diving, and exploring the vibrant marine life. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are also home to several indigenous tribes and offer a unique cultural experience.

Places to Stay: Havelock Island Beach Resort, Port Blair Hotel

Things To Do: Visit Radhanagar Beach, Explore the Cellular Jail, Take a boat tour to the nearby islands, Enjoy water sports activities

Best Time to Visit: November to April

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Veer Savarkar International Airport in Port Blair, which is the main gateway to the Andaman and Nicobar Islands. The islands can also be reached by ferry from the mainland, with regular services from Chennai, Kolkata, and Vishakhapatnam.

Ghats, Varanasi – The Realm Of Spirituality And Divinity

The Ghats of Varanasi are a series of steps leading down to the banks of the River Ganges in Varanasi, India. They are considered one of the most sacred places in Hinduism and are a major pilgrimage site for devotees. The Ghats are known for their spiritual significance and are often used for religious rituals, including bathing in the river, performing ceremonies, and cremation. Visitors can witness the vibrant culture and spirituality of Varanasi while exploring the Ghats and experiencing the unique atmosphere of this ancient city.

Places to Stay: Hotel Alka, BrijRama Palace

Things To Do: Visit the Dashashwamedh Ghat, Explore the Manikarnika Ghat, Take a boat ride on the River Ganges, Witness the Ganga Aarti ceremony

Best Time to Visit: October to March

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport, which is around 26 km from Varanasi. The nearest railway station is Varanasi Junction, which is well-connected to major cities across India.

Tirupati can be called “Where Heaven Meets Earth” because millions of devotees believe the divine presence of Tirumala Venkateswara Temple brings a heavenly spiritual experience to people on earth. The peaceful hills, sacred atmosphere, powerful devotion, temple traditions, and strong emotional connection people feel there make it seem like a place where the spiritual world and human world come together.

Places to Stay: Hotel Bliss, Fortune Select Grand Ridge

Things To Do: Visit the Tirumala Venkateswara Temple, Explore the Sri Venkateswara National Park, Take a walk through the Alipiri Footsteps, Enjoy the scenic beauty of the surrounding hills

Best Time to Visit: Any time of the year

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Tirupati Airport, which is around 15 km from Tirupati. The nearest railway station is Tirupati Railway Station, which is well-connected to major cities across India.

Caves, Meghalaya – Pothole Of The East

Meghalaya, often referred to as the "Pothole of the East," is renowned for its extensive network of caves. These caves are formed by the unique geological formations in the region and are a major attraction for adventure enthusiasts and nature lovers. The caves in Meghalaya offer a thrilling experience of exploring underground passages, stalactites, and stalagmites. Some of the popular caves include Mawsmai Cave, Liat Prah Cave, and Siju Cave. Visitors can enjoy spelunking and witness the fascinating rock formations while exploring these natural wonders.

Places to Stay: Shillong Hotel, Pinewood Hotel

Things To Do: Visit the Mawsmai Cave, Explore the Liat Prah Cave, Take a tour of the Siju Cave, Enjoy the scenic beauty of the surrounding hills

Best Time to Visit: September to May

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Shillong Airport, which is around 85 km from Meghalaya. The nearest railway station is Guwahati Railway Station, which is around 150 km from Meghalaya.

Tawang, Arunachal Pradesh – The Calm Monasteries

Tawang is a town located in the Arunachal Pradesh state of India. It is known for its calm monasteries and stunning natural beauty. The town is home to the Tawang Monastery, which is one of the largest Buddhist monasteries in India and a significant pilgrimage site for Buddhists. Tawang offers a serene and peaceful atmosphere, making it an ideal destination for those seeking spiritual solace and a connection with nature. Visitors can explore the monasteries, enjoy the scenic landscapes, and experience the unique culture of the region.

Places to Stay: Tawang View Hotel, Hotel Gakyin

Things To Do: Visit the Tawang Monastery, Explore the Bum La Pass, Take a tour of the Sela Pass, Enjoy the scenic beauty of the surrounding mountains

Best Time to Visit: April to October

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Tezpur Airport, which is around 170 km from Tawang. The nearest railway station is Rangapara North Railway Station, which is around 150 km from Tawang.

Chilka Lake, Odisha – The Unexplored, Hidden Gem Of The East

Chilka Lake is a brackish water lagoon located in the state of Odisha, India. It is the largest coastal lagoon in India and the second largest in the world. The lake is known for its rich biodiversity and is a haven for birdwatchers and nature enthusiasts. Chilka Lake is home to a wide variety of flora and fauna, including migratory birds, dolphins, and various fish species. Visitors can take boat rides on the lake to explore its beauty and witness the diverse wildlife that inhabits the area.

Places to Stay: Chilika Lake Resort, Hotel Sea View

Things To Do: Take a boat ride on Chilika Lake, Explore the Nalabana Bird Sanctuary, Visit the Kalijai Temple, Enjoy the scenic beauty of the surrounding landscapes

Best Time to Visit: November to February

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Biju Patnaik International Airport in Bhubaneswar, which is around 120 km from Chilika Lake. The nearest railway station is Khurda Road Junction, which is around 100 km from Chilika Lake.

Rann Of Kutch – Salt Desert Of The West

The Rann of Kutch is a salt desert located in the state of Gujarat, India. It is known for its vast expanse of white salt flats that stretch as far as the eye can see. The Rann of Kutch is a unique and surreal landscape that attracts tourists from around the world. The best time to visit the Rann of Kutch is during the Rann Utsav, a cultural festival that takes place from November to February, where visitors can experience the vibrant culture, traditional music, dance, and local cuisine of the region.

Places to Stay: Rann Riders, The Rann Resort

Things To Do: Visit the Great Rann of Kutch, Explore the Kalo Dungar (Black Hill), Take a tour of the Dholavira archaeological site, Enjoy the cultural festivities during the Rann Utsav

Best Time to Visit: November to February

How To Reach: The nearest airport is Bhuj Airport, which is around 90 km from the Rann of Kutch. The nearest railway station is Bhuj Railway Station, which is well-connected to major cities across India.
